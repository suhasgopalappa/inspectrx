What InspectRx Catches

Common hospital billing tricks that cost you thousands

MRP Violations

Medications charged above MRP is illegal under India's DPCO. We catch every instance.

Double Billing

Same test or item appearing twice on your bill? InspectRx flags duplicate charges instantly.

Inflated Consumables

Gloves at 5x market rate? Syringes at 4x? We compare against known market prices.

Room Charge Padding

Billed for a private room when you were in a general ward? We spot category mismatches.
